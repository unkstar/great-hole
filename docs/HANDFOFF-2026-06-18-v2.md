### Critical bug fix in LuaLib
`fec_shared_state_new()` must call `interface.Schedule()` to balance `lua_yield` in `safe_yield` wrapper. Without it, the Lua coroutine suspends forever → FiberException crash.

### Round 2: BF1-BF6 all enabled

| Tunnel | TCP DL | TCP UL | Retr | Cwnd |
|--------|--------|--------|:---:|------|
| hole | 44.2/42.6M | 64.9/65.2M | 214/11998 | 283-615KB |
| fec (old) | 24.0/21.7M | 13.8/12.1M | 1/488 | 536-950KB |

**Key takeaway**: UL improvement is consistent across both runs. DL improvement from Round 1 may have been masked by network variance in Round 2. BF6 (PING/FEEDBACK) adds RTT measurement loop but adaptive overhead not yet wired — RTT only used for decode timeout.
